🎮 SlideQuest Guide & Controls

1

1. How to Play the Sliding Puzzle

Reorder the scrambled tiles in numerical sequence from 1 to the end, leaving the bottom-right space empty. Only tiles adjacent to the empty cell can move.

2

2. Keyboard & Touch Controls

Click or tap any tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it. You can also use Arrow Keys or WASD to shift tiles into the empty space.

3

3. Enable Camera

Click Enable Camera. Video is processed purely in your browser in volatile memory—no footage is ever uploaded or saved.

4

4. Mode A: Directional Swipes

Point with your index finger. Quickly flick your finger UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT to slide a tile into the empty space. Return to center before making another move.

👆 ➡️ 🎯
5

5. Mode B: Pinch & Drag

Hover your index fingertip cursor over a tile. Pinch your thumb and index together to grab, drag over to the glowing empty space, and open fingers to drop.

👌 🟦 ➡️ ⬛
6

6. Switch Anytime

Switch between Normal, Mode A, and Mode B anytime without losing your puzzle progress! Standard mouse and keyboard controls always remain active.
